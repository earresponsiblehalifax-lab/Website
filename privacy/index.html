Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, how this website handles personal information. It is not legal advice.

Who we are

Ear Responsible is operated by 15933803 Canada Inc. in Halifax, Nova Scotia. Questions about your information: earresponsiblehalifax@gmail.com or 506 260 4545.

Orders

Checkout is handled by Stripe. When you buy earplugs, Stripe collects what it needs to take payment and ship the order, such as your name, email, shipping address, and payment details. We receive the order details Stripe shares with us so we can pack the order, ship it, and answer questions about it.

Messages

If you email us, call us, or use the contact form, we receive what you send, including your name, email address, and message. The contact form opens your email app and addresses the message to earresponsiblehalifax@gmail.com. We read those messages in that inbox.

What this site does not do

This site does not run analytics or advertising, and it does not ask you to create an account. It does not set its own cookies.

Shipping

Canada Post receives the name and address needed to deliver the parcel, whether you choose untracked Lettermail or tracked shipping.

How we use it

We use this information to fill orders, ship within Canada, handle returns, and reply to you. We do not sell personal information.
