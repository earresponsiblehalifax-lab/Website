FAQ

How much noise do they block?

They're rated NRR 23 dB.

Will I still hear the music?

Yes. The hi-fi filters lower the volume and keep the music clear.

Are they comfortable for long wear?

They're soft silicone and one size fits all. People wear them at festivals, shows, and loud workplaces.

Can I sleep in them?

Yes. They're made of soft silicone, so they're comfortable to sleep in.

How do I fit them?

They're one size fits all. If they don't feel right, email us and we'll help.

How do I clean them?

Take out the filter, then wash the silicone in hot, soapy water.

Are they OK for kids?

We don't have age or sizing guidance for children. Please email us before buying a pair for a child.

What do I get?

One pair of hi-fi earplugs and a keychain case.

How much are they?

$20 CAD, plus HST at checkout.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere in Canada.

What are the shipping options?
  • Lettermail (untracked): $7, usually 5–10 business days. If it gets lost, we'll send it again.
  • Tracked Canada Post: $15, usually 3–7 business days, with a tracking number.

HST added at checkout.

Can I return them?

Yes, within 30 days. Email us, call us, or see the Returns page. You pay return shipping, unless the item is defective or we sent the wrong one. Then we cover it.

Do you do bulk, custom, or event orders?

Email us or use the contact form and tell us what you need.
