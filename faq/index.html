FAQ

What noise reduction do the earplugs provide?

The official rating is 23 dB.

Will I still hear the music?

Our HiFi Filters are designed to preserve the integrity of sound while reducing the intensity of harmful frequencies, ensuring you can fully immerse yourself in the music without sacrificing your hearing health.

Are they comfortable at festivals or for long wear?

Made from premium silicone, our earplugs offer a perfect fit every time. The soft, flexible material conforms to your ear shape, providing unparalleled comfort during extended wear. Experience an irritation-free auditory experience, whether you're at a music festival, sporting event, or even catching some Z's in a noisy environment.

Can I sleep in them?

They are soft silicone and are meant for extended wear, including sleep in a noisy environment.
